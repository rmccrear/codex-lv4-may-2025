## Overview

Practice your JavaScript skills by completing the free lessons and challenges on [Learn JavaScript Online](https://learnjavascript.online/). This interactive platform offers hands-on practice with modern JavaScript concepts in a browser-based environment.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Complete Free Lessons

Visit [https://learnjavascript.online/](https://learnjavascript.online/) and complete **all the free assignments** available in the course.

The free tier includes:
- First 77 lessons
- Challenges
- Projects (first 7 chapters)
- Flashcards

Work through these at your own pace. The interactive environment allows you to practice directly in your browser.

### Step 2: Take a Screenshot

Once you've completed all the free assignments, take **one screenshot** showing your progress. This could be:
- Your progress dashboard
- The completion status of the free chapters
- A specific lesson or challenge you completed

### Step 3: Reflection

In your submission, include a brief reflection with:

1. **One thing you learned**: Share a specific JavaScript concept, technique, or insight that was new or particularly helpful to you.

2. **One thing you would like to know more about**: Identify a topic or concept from the course that you'd like to explore further or dive deeper into.

## Submission

Submit your screenshot and reflection as your assignment submission.

**Note**: This is a practice assignment designed to reinforce your JavaScript fundamentals. Take your time and focus on understanding the concepts rather than rushing through.
